# Public Validation Changelog

## 2026.v1 — Weeks 1-3

The original 2026 Product A release governs the frozen Weeks 1-3 receipts.

### Week 1 receipt designation

The designated Week 1 receipt is version 2. Its designation records the receipt it
superseded. The canonical final-public artifact and both hashes remain documented.

### Week 2 operational correction

Week 2 receipt version 1 omitted rebuilt rolling Control Rate and ADV Drive
Conversion derivatives after Week 1 scoring. Version 2 restored the already frozen
blend and was designated before the affected games. The model specification did not
change. The original receipt and designation remain under `week-02/superseded/v1/`.

## 2026.v2 — Week 4 forward

The model changed prospectively at Week 4 after defects were found in sparse-season
opponent adjustment and prior construction. Published Weeks 1-3 were not rewritten.
The public record therefore labels 2026 a development-validation season rather than
a clean single-specification validation season.

The independent replication outputs separate results by release as well as reporting
the combined public record. Combined totals describe everything published; release
splits are required when evaluating a particular specification.
